Drones are an amazing way to learn about control algorithms. 
<br><br>
A popular algorithm in robotics and engineering in general is PID. PID stands for Proportional, Integral, Derivative. It's an algorithm which uses those three operations to reduce error in your system. 
<br><br>
Your system, be it a thermostat or a drone, is looking to reach some sort of target. A thermostat will stabilize to set the temperature a user requests. A drone will stabilize around a height that the user commands.
<br><br>
How exactly does this stabilization around this setpoint occur? With a control algorithm, such as PID.
