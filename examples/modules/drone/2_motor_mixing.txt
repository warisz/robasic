Cool, now let's learn how to control the drone to fly in commanded directions. 
We will use a popular motor mixing algorithm, which implements PID on every axis. 
<br>
<br>
For the four propellers: 
<br>
T1 = Throttle + Roll - Pitch + Yaw 
<br>
T2 = Throttle - Roll - Pitch - Yaw
<br>
T3 = Throttle - Roll + Pitch + Yaw
<br>
T4 = Throttle + Roll + Pitch - Yaw
